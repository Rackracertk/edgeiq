# NBA / NFL starter-key verification
The manual GitHub Actions workflow on main verifies the secret without consuming credits on workflow-file pushes. For publishing, select NBA or NFL, optionally enter a team name, and confirm display rights. At most two upcoming games within 48 hours, three markets per game, US region, at most six credits per run. No public user can initiate a paid provider request. The public snapshot is limited to matched fixtures and quotes under 15 minutes old; stale lines disappear rather than being misrepresented. This is a manual beta test, not continuous odds coverage. Do not enable the old multi-sport odds publisher on a starter key without budgeting its calls. Provider terms permit analytical websites provided raw data is not the primary product sold or redistributed: https://the-odds-api.com/terms-and-conditions.html. Never commit the key or place it in a VITE variable.
